03 · Layout, radii, elevation
Header — safe-area top + 44px bar, 16px side padding, logo tile 28×28 r6, hamburger 44×44 hit area.
Toolbar — 44px tall, 8px side padding, 6px gap. Chips 30px tall, 9px side padding, r6, bg white 10%, border white 16%. Search fills remaining width.
Timeline panel — floats over the map 8px below the toolbar, 6px side margin, r10, padding 8/10/6, three rows (title · track · days), gap 6.

Floating panels — r8, bg #0E2029 @ 92%, padding 8/10, shadow 0 4 14 black 35%. Layer card top-left, layers button (40×40, r8, 14% border) top-right, both 8px from the timeline.

Callouts — 24px tall, r4, 8px side padding, anchored 12px below-right of the pin, bg #0E2029 @ 92%, shadow 0 3 10 black 40%.
Gradients — swell: #64D5CC → #4798B7 40% → #2B5BC7 75% → #163797. Wind: #27A055 → #A8C24A 45% → #F0A24A 75% → #E66729. Bars 8px, r4.
Touch — every tappable thing has a ≥44px hit area even where the visual is 24–30px.

05 · Map layer stack (bottom → top)
0
Satellite basemap — true-color imagery, no roads/POI labels. Camera locked to a NE-coast bounding box (Kennebunkport → Montauk), pan/zoom within it.
1
Swell-quality raster — smooth-edged zones over water only (clip to ocean mask): green #41C776, peach #F3C79E, grey #9C9EA1 at ~65% opacity, 40px feathered edges. Land is never tinted.
2
Wind streamlines — particle/flow layer, 1px cyan #64D5CC lines, 70% opacity, fading tails, follow the wind vector field; ocean only.
3
Swell rings — one pulse per active break, radius scaled to swell height, color = quality.
4
Break markers + labels — pin left of a plated uppercase label; collision-avoid labels at low zoom (drop the least-ranked first).
5
Callouts + zone labels — plated text, never overlap a marker.
6
Chrome — header, toolbar, timeline, layer card, layers button, legends, hotspot card. Fixed; the map scrolls under them.
06 · Behavior
Timeline — drag the knob or tap a day/chevron. Selected day re-renders layers 1–3 and both callouts; date label updates; segments up to the selected day fill with accent. Snap to whole days; 200ms ease on the knob.
Pins — tap: open that break's callout, dim other rings to 40%. Tap map: close. Long-press: nothing (no hidden gestures).
Hotspot card — always shows the #1 ranked break for the selected day. Tapping it flies the camera to that pin.
Toolbar modes — Forecast slider = this screen. Refraction map swaps layer 1 for a refraction/bathymetry raster. Live buoy feed swaps rings for buoy markers with live readings. Chrome never changes between modes.
Search — type-ahead over break names; selecting one flies to it and opens its callout.
Empty / loading — map tiles first, then zones fade in (300ms), then streamlines start. Never show a spinner over the map; show a thin accent progress line under the toolbar.
Do not — add a light theme, a status-bar mock, drop shadows on text, or any panel wider than 168px on a 390 screen. Map must stay ≥ 60% of the visible height.
